Our Founding Story

Born from a love
for the extraordinary

LIORA was born in 2025, from a single belief: that true luxury is not about excess — it is the quiet certainty of something made exactly right.

I'm Nafeesa Farsana. At 18, I built LIORA from the ground up — not just as a store, but as a statement. Every handbag we carry is chosen with care, every detail considered, every customer treated as someone who deserves something extraordinary.

What started as a passion project became a full e-commerce platform — designed, developed, and launched entirely by me, with every feature built to give our customers the finest shopping experience possible.
